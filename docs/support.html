Support

How can we help?

Most OpenUntil features work offline. These quick answers cover the things people ask about most.

Contact support

Email hellojiawen123@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version, OpenUntil version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include sensitive product or health information.

Why did I not receive a reminder?

Open OpenUntil → Settings and check that Notifications is On. Also check iOS Settings → Notifications → OpenUntil. The app sends one daily summary at the time you choose rather than a separate alert for every item.

What does “Check by” mean?

It is a prompt to review the product, not a guarantee that something is safe or unsafe. Always follow the printed label, storage instructions, and your judgment.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, choose Add Widget, search for OpenUntil, select a size, and add it. The widget shows up to three upcoming items and a quick-add link.

How do backups work?

In Settings → Your data, use Export JSON backup for a restorable copy. CSV is intended for viewing in a spreadsheet and cannot be imported. Keep exported files somewhere you trust.

Does iCloud sync replace a backup?

No. iCloud sync is convenient for keeping your own devices aligned, but a separate JSON backup is the safer recovery copy.

What is included for free?

You can keep up to five active items without purchasing. The one-time lifetime unlock removes the active-item limit. There is no subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings, choose the lifetime unlock area, and use Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Can I recover a deleted item?

Deleted items remain in Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days. Finished or discarded items remain in History and can be added again from there.

OpenUntil does not provide medical, food-safety, or product-safety advice. Follow the product label and professional guidance where appropriate.

联系支持

为什么没有收到提醒？

进入 OpenUntil → 设置，确认通知状态为“已开启”；同时检查 iOS 设置 → 通知 → OpenUntil。App 每天只在你选择的时间发送一条汇总，不会为每件物品连续推送。

“检查日期”代表什么？

它只是提醒你查看产品，并不保证物品安全或不安全。请始终遵循包装标签、储存说明并自行判断。

怎样添加小组件？

长按主屏幕空白处，选择添加小组件，搜索 OpenUntil，选择尺寸后添加。小组件会显示最近三件物品和快速添加入口。

备份怎样使用？

在设置 → 你的数据中，选择导出 JSON 备份即可生成可恢复的副本。CSV 用于在表格中查看，不能导入。请把导出的文件保存在可信位置。

iCloud 同步能代替备份吗？

不能。iCloud 适合让自己的设备保持同步，但单独保存一份 JSON 备份更适合用于恢复。

免费版包含什么？

不购买也可以同时保留最多 5 件活动物品。一次性终身解锁会移除活动物品数量限制，无订阅。

怎样恢复购买？

进入设置，打开终身解锁页面并选择“恢复购买”。请确认设备登录的是当时购买所用的 Apple 账户。

误删的物品可以恢复吗？

删除的物品会在设置 → 最近删除中保留 30 天。已完成或丢弃的物品会保留在历史记录中，也可以再次添加。
