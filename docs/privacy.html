Privacy Policy

OpenUntil is designed to work without an account or a developer-operated server.

Effective: October 9, 2026

In short

OpenUntil does not sell personal information, show ads, use analytics SDKs, or track you across apps and websites. The developer does not receive the item names, dates, notes, reminders, backups, or exports that you create in the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter, including item names, categories, opened dates, check-by dates, optional package dates, notes, reminder choices, completion history, and recently deleted records. This information supports the features you choose to use and remains on your device unless you enable optional iCloud sync or export it.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy of supported app data is stored through Apple's iCloud key-value service under your Apple Account so your devices can stay in sync. OpenUntil does not operate the iCloud service and does not receive that synced content. Turning sync off stops further app-initiated syncing; data already held by Apple may remain subject to your Apple Account and Apple's policies.

Widget and notifications

The widget receives a small local snapshot through an Apple App Group shared container. Notifications are scheduled on your device. Neither feature sends your item data to an OpenUntil server.

Purchases

Lifetime unlock purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. OpenUntil checks the purchase entitlement supplied by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Exports and imports

JSON backups and CSV exports are created only when you request them. You choose where to save or share exported files. Imported JSON files are read to restore or merge your data on the device.

Data collection and tracking

OpenUntil does not collect data for advertising, marketing, analytics, profiling, or tracking. Apple may process App Store, iCloud, and notification service data under Apple's own terms.

Children

OpenUntil is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its store listing where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hellojiawen123@gmail.com or through the OpenUntil Support page.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

简要说明

OpenUntil 不出售个人信息、不展示广告、不使用分析 SDK，也不会跨 App 或网站追踪你。开发者不会收到你在 App 中创建的物品名称、日期、备注、提醒、备份或导出内容。

设备本地保存的信息

App 会保存你输入的物品名称、分类、开封日期、检查日期、可选包装日期、备注、提醒设置、完成历史和最近删除记录。这些内容用于你选择使用的功能；除非你主动开启 iCloud 同步或执行导出，否则它们保留在设备本地。

可选 iCloud 同步

开启 iCloud 同步后，App 会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储，在你的 Apple 账户下保存受支持数据的副本，以便设备之间同步。OpenUntil 不运营 iCloud 服务，也不会收到同步内容。关闭同步会停止 App 后续发起的同步；Apple 已保存的数据仍可能按照你的 Apple 账户设置和 Apple 政策保留。

小组件与通知

小组件通过 Apple 的 App Group 共享容器读取少量本地摘要；通知在设备本地安排。这两项功能都不会把物品数据发送到 OpenUntil 服务器。

购买

终身解锁由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。OpenUntil 只检查 Apple 提供的购买权益，开发者不会收到你的银行卡信息。

导出与导入

只有在你主动操作时，App 才会生成 JSON 备份或 CSV 文件；保存和分享位置由你选择。导入 JSON 时，文件仅用于在设备上恢复或合并数据。

数据收集与追踪

OpenUntil 不为广告、营销、分析、画像或追踪目的收集数据。Apple 可能依据其自身条款处理 App Store、iCloud 与通知服务相关数据。

儿童

OpenUntil 是通用工具，并非面向儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策发生变化，上方生效日期会同步更新；重大变化会酌情在 App 或商店页面中说明。
